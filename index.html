MeetMind*

MeetMind is an AI that attends your meetings so you don't have to remember them. It extracts personalized action items, priorities, and deadlines for every single attendee within seconds.

How It Works

From raw meeting audio to personalized action items in four simple steps.

1

Input Your Meeting

Paste a transcript, upload an audio/video file, or record live directly in your browser using the Web Speech API.

2

AI Analyzes

Gemini 2.0 Flash processes your transcript, attributes every statement to a speaker, and extracts action items with source quotes.

3

Personalized Cards

Each attendee gets their own card with tasks, priorities, deadlines, and dependencies. Nothing generic — everything attributed.

4

Export & Share

Copy to clipboard, share via WhatsApp with markdown formatting, export as PDF, or preview follow-up emails per attendee.
